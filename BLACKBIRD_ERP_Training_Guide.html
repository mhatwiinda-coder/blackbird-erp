1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE

What is BLACKBIRD ERP?

BLACKBIRD ERP is a comprehensive management system designed for logistics and transportation companies. It provides real-time tracking of drivers, vehicles, payments, and operations with role-based access control. The system has three main components:

Component Description
Public Website Customer-facing portal for service quotations and booking
Driver Portal Individual driver login for payment submissions, earnings, and RTO agreements
ERP Dashboard Administrative system for managers, accountants, and leadership with analytics and reporting

Key Features

  • Real-Time Dashboard: Live metrics on drivers, vehicles, revenue, and alerts
  • Driver Management: Track all active drivers with status, documents, and assignments
  • Fleet Management: Vehicle inventory, maintenance tracking, insurance/tax due dates
  • Payment Processing: Record driver payments, invoices, and RTO agreements
  • RTO (Rent-to-Own): Manage vehicle purchase agreements with automatic ownership transfer
  • Driver Submissions: Drivers submit payments, secretary approves for recording
  • Weekly Logs: Track driver income, mileage, and trip data
  • Analytics: Revenue tracking, driver performance, fleet utilization reports
  • Role-Based Access: Different views and permissions for each user role

3. DRIVER PORTAL (DRIVER SELF-SERVICE)

Driver Portal Overview

The Driver Portal is a dedicated system where individual drivers can manage their account, view earnings, and submit payment records. This portal is separate from the ERP system and provides drivers with transparency on their financial records.

Driver Dashboard Features

After logging in, drivers see:

Feature Description
Welcome Banner Displays driver name and login status
Total Submitted Sum of all payments submitted this month
Pending Submissions Count of submissions awaiting approval
Approved This Month Count of approved payments this month
Submit New Payment Form to submit weekly cashing amount
Submission History Table showing all past submissions with status
Status Indicators Pending (orange), Approved (green), Rejected (red)
[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER: Driver Portal Dashboard]

How to Submit a Payment

Step 1: On the Driver Dashboard, locate the "Submit New Payment" section
Step 2: Fill in the following fields:
  • Amount: The cash amount collected (required)
  • Week: The week number (auto-filled to current week)
  • Month: The month (auto-filled to current month)
  • Notes/Remarks: Optional details about the payment
Step 3: Click "Submit Payment"
Step 4: Your submission will appear in "Submission History" with status "Pending"
Step 5: Wait for Secretary/HR to review and approve
Step 6: Once approved, status changes to "Approved" and payment is recorded in ERP
[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER: Driver - Submit New Payment Form]
[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER: Driver - Submission History with Status]

Viewing Payment History

The "Submission History" table shows all your past submissions with:

  • Date Submitted: When you submitted the payment
  • Amount: The payment amount
  • Status: Pending, Approved, or Rejected
  • Week/Month: The period for this payment
  • Approved Date: When it was approved (if approved)
  • Rejection Reason: Why it was rejected (if rejected)

You can filter by status and search for specific submissions.

4. ERP SYSTEM - CEO (READ-ONLY ACCESS)

CEO Role Overview

The CEO role provides read-only access to all company data for decision-making and oversight. CEOs can view reports, dashboards, and analytics but cannot modify any data. This ensures data integrity and creates an audit trail.

CEO Access Restrictions

CEOs CAN:
  • View the company dashboard with all metrics and KPIs
  • View all drivers, vehicles, jobs, and deliveries
  • View payment records and financial data
  • Export data to Excel
  • View analytics and reports
  • Monitor real-time alerts and vehicle status
CEOs CANNOT:
  • Create, edit, or delete any records
  • Process payments or invoices
  • Assign drivers to vehicles
  • Approve RTO agreements
  • Change system settings

6. ERP SYSTEM - EXECUTIVE SECRETARY

Secretary Role Overview

The Executive Secretary role manages financial operations, approves payment submissions, and handles quotations. Secretaries process driver payments and manage customer interactions.

Secretary Access Permissions

Secretaries CAN:
  • View all driver information (read-only)
  • View job and delivery assignments
  • APPROVE driver payment submissions
  • REJECT driver payment submissions
  • Record driver payments
  • Manage quotations (create, convert to jobs)
  • Create and manage RTO agreements
  • APPROVE RTO driver payments
  • View and export reports
  • Monitor pending approvals

Key Secretary Functions

  • RTO Driver Submissions: Review and approve/reject driver RTO payment submissions
  • Pending RTO Approvals: Queue of RTO payments waiting for secretary approval
  • Quotations: Create and manage customer quotations
  • Payments: Record payments and manage payment records
  • Rent-to-Own: Manage RTO agreements and track payment progress
  • Dashboard: Overview of pending approvals and key metrics

How to Approve Driver Submissions

Step 1: From the main menu, click "RTO Driver Submissions" or "Pending RTO Approvals"
Step 2: Review the pending submissions table showing:
  • Driver name and ID
  • Amount submitted
  • Submission date and period (week/month)
  • Notes from driver
Step 3: Click "REVIEW & APPROVE" button on the submission
Step 4: A modal appears showing full submission details
Step 5: Either:
  • Click "APPROVE" to accept and record the payment
  • Click "REJECT" to deny and provide rejection reason
Step 6: Payment status updates in real-time
  • Approved: Payment recorded in ERP, driver notified
  • Rejected: Driver can resubmit, reason provided

9. STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOWS & PROCEDURES

Workflow 1: Driver Payment Submission & Approval

Actors: Driver, Secretary/HR Manager

1. Driver logs in to driver portal (http://localhost:8000/driver-login)
2. Driver submits payment
  • Enters amount collected (e.g., 500 ZMW)
  • Week/month auto-filled
  • Optional notes (e.g., "Collected from fares")
  • Clicks "Submit Payment"
3. Driver sees submission in history with status "Pending"
4. Secretary/HR receives notification with link to submissions
5. Secretary reviews in "RTO Driver Submissions" section
6. Secretary approves or rejects
  • If approved: Payment recorded in Payments table, status → "Approved"
  • If rejected: Status → "Rejected" with reason
7. Driver sees updated status in their portal
Time to complete: 5-10 minutes

Workflow 2: Rent-to-Own (RTO) Vehicle Agreement

Actors: HR Manager, Secretary, Driver

1. HR Manager creates RTO Agreement
  • Goes to "Rent-to-Own" section → Click "New Agreement"
  • Selects driver (customer) and vehicle
  • Sets total price and down payment
  • Clicks "Create Agreement"
2. System initializes agreement with remaining balance calculation
3. Driver makes payments via driver portal
  • Driver submits payment → Secretary approves → Payment recorded
4. Secretary records payments in RTO section
  • Goes to "Rent-to-Own" → Click "Record Payment"
  • Selects agreement and payment amount
  • Clicks "Record Payment"
5. System updates balance in real-time
6. Automatic completion when balance reaches 0
  • Status changes to "Completed"
  • Ownership auto-transfers to driver
  • Timestamp recorded
7. Driver now owns vehicle
Time to complete: Weeks/months depending on payment schedule

Workflow 3: Onboarding a New Driver

Actors: HR Manager

1. HR Manager adds new driver
  • Goes to "Drivers & Riders" → Click "+ Add Driver"
  • Fills in: Full name, Phone, Vehicle plate, Type, National ID, License number
  • Clicks "Save Driver"
2. Driver automatically registered in system
3. HR assigns vehicle (optional)
  • Selects driver → Clicks "Edit"
  • Assigns vehicle from dropdown
  • Clicks "Save"
4. Driver portal account auto-created on first login
  • Password: FirstName@123 (e.g., Edward@123)
5. Driver can now
  • Log in to driver portal
  • Submit payments
  • View their records
Time to complete: 5 minutes

Workflow 4: Generating Monthly Revenue Report (Accountant)

Actors: Company Accountant

1. Accountant logs in to ERP (http://localhost:8000/erp)
2. Navigates to Analytics section
3. Views Monthly Revenue chart showing current month earnings
4. Goes to Payments section to see detailed transaction list
5. Filters by date range (optional)
  • Uses date picker to select start/end date
  • Views all payments in that period
6. Exports to Excel
  • Clicks "Export" button
  • File downloads with all payment details
7. Uses for accounting
  • Reconciles with bank statements
  • Prepares financial reports
  • Auditing and compliance
Time to complete: 10-15 minutes

10. TROUBLESHOOTING & SUPPORT

Common Issues & Solutions

Q: Driver cannot login

A: Verify driver ID is correct and password is FirstName@123. System auto-creates account on first login.

Q: Password incorrect error

A: Password is case-sensitive. Default is FirstName@123 (capitalize first name). Contact HR to reset.

Q: Payment submission not showing

A: Refresh page (Ctrl+F5). Payment appears immediately after submission.

Q: Approval button not visible

A: Check that you have Secretary or HR role. Only these roles can approve.

Q: Cannot edit vehicle

A: Click the Edit button on the vehicle row. Form opens in modal. Update fields and click Save.

Q: RTO agreement not calculating

A: Ensure driver and vehicle are selected. System auto-calculates remaining balance.

Q: Export to Excel not working

A: Try different browser or clear cache. Ensure data is loaded before exporting.

Q: Cannot see all menu items

A: Check your user role. Different roles have different menu access.

Q: System seems slow

A: Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete). Sync service runs every 5 minutes.

Q: Data not updating

A: Refresh page (Ctrl+F5) or restart server if self-hosted.

Support & Contact

For technical issues or questions:

  • Check this training guide for step-by-step instructions
  • Review the role-specific sections for your user type
  • Clear browser cache and try again (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
  • Restart your browser
  • Contact system administrator for account or permission issues
Key System Details:
• ERP System: http://localhost:8000/erp (or Netlify URL)
• Driver Portal: http://localhost:8000/driver-login
• Backend: Node.js/Express
• Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL) with SQLite sync
• Auto-sync: Every 5 minutes for vehicles and drivers
